Readers can suggest a fix to a public article. Nothing changes in Git until a member holding `PUBLISH` accepts it.

**Readers.** Signed-in community members get 「建议修改」 below a public article:

- they edit the served body and may add a reason;
- the proposal carries a SHA-256 of the body they started from;
- one open proposal per reader and article, 5 a minute and 30 a day, and no proposal across a block with an owner;
- they can withdraw an open proposal, and are notified when it is accepted, declined or found stale.

**Reviewers.** A new 「读者勘误」 studio section lists open proposals with a line diff.

- Accept re-reads the file at the remote head through the authorized reader.
- Only if the body still matches the base does it replace the body. Every byte before the body is kept, including a BOM and CRLF frontmatter.
- The commit carries `Poketto-Suggested-By: account:<id>`. `RepositoryPatch` gains an optional `suggestedBy` for this.
- Otherwise the proposal becomes STALE. Decline changes nothing.

**Credit.** Accepted proposers are thanked in the article footer, unless they and an owner have blocked each other.

**Schema.** `V22`:

- the `community_corrections` table;
- `community_notifications` rows can now reference a correction event instead of a comment.

It needs `V21` from #200, so #200 merges first.

**Deviations from the proposal.** The note records them:

- proposals are not reportable to site administrators, because only the space's reviewers ever see them;
- resolved text is cleared rather than the rows deleted, so credit and inbox history remain; the clearing runs when a new proposal is stored.

**Tests.**

- `CorrectionsIntegrationIT` on PostgreSQL: refusals, notifications, `PUBLISH` gating, accept/decline/stale, credit and blocking.
- `RepositoryReviewedBodyEditsTests` against a real remote: bytes preserved, trailers, already-applied, stale.
- Body filter limit test.
- Frontend tests: digest, proposal with CRLF, review with diff and confirmation, notification wording.

Note moved to `notes/implemented/`; both usage docs updated.
