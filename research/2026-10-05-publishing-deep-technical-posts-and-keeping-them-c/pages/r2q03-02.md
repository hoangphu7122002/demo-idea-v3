## What is wrong

`blog_post.updated_at` is bumped on every row update (`$onUpdate` in `packages/db/src/schema/blog/blog-post.table.ts`). The public views endpoint (`apps/web/app/api/blog/posts/[id]/views/route.ts`) increments `views` on that same row, so every page view rewrites `updated_at`. That column is then used as:

- `<lastmod>` in `apps/web/app/sitemap/blog.xml/route.ts`
- `dateModified` in the article JSON-LD and `article:modified_time` in Open Graph (`app/[slug]/page.tsx`, `app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx`, `components/blog/blog-post-content.component.tsx`)
- the "Updated on" line shown to readers (`getMeaningfulUpdateDate`)
- `staleness.lastUpdatedAt` / `ageMonths` in the refresh brief and decay detection (`apps/admin/lib/services/refresh-brief.service.ts`, `decay-detection.service.ts`)

Evidence from production (2026-09-11): 25 published posts report a modification in the last 7 days and 115 in the second half of August, while `blog_post_revision` has zero rows in that period. The posts "modified" most recently are exactly the most-read ones. Search Console shows the sitemap re-downloaded daily.

Effects: Google learns the sitemap's lastmod is noise and ignores it (so a real refresh loses its recrawl signal); structured data claims daily updates that did not happen; the refresh loop believes the most valuable posts were just touched and under-prioritises them.

## Design

Add a dedicated content timestamp and let the database maintain it, so none of the ~35 code paths that update `blog_post` can get it wrong:

- New column `blog_post.content_updated_at timestamp` (nullable).
- A `BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE` trigger sets it to `now()` only when a reader-visible column actually changes: `title, content, meta_title, meta_description, excerpt, quick_answer, faqs, featured_image_id, slug`. Bookkeeping columns (`views`, `likes`, `shares`, pipeline/processing state, `planning_data`, `pipeline_state`, `idea_approval`, `priority`) never touch it.
- Backfill: `COALESCE(max(blog_post_revision.created_at), published_at, created_at)`.
- Readers switch to `GREATEST(published_at, content_updated_at)`: sitemap lastmod, `dateModified`, `article:modified_time`, the "Updated on" line, the refresh brief's staleness and decay detection.
- The views endpoint preserves `updated_at` explicitly so public traffic stops churning the row timestamp at all; `updated_at` stays what it is, generic row bookkeeping for the admin.

This is the first trigger in the schema. It is documented in the table file and `packages/db/README.md`; `drizzle-kit generate` does not manage it, the migration carries it as hand-written SQL after the generated `ALTER TABLE`.

## Out of scope

Moving `views/likes/shares` to their own table (would also be correct, larger change, not needed once lastmod reads the dedicated column).

Found during the Search Console audit of 2026-09-11.
